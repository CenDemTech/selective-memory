          The sensitivity label determines whether the conversation contains potentially sensitive data. The label is generated by rule-based logic using RegEx run by a local script when your data is uploaded. No LLM or classifier calls are made. It scans the whole conversation, your messages and the assistant's replies.
          • Green: nothing flagged.
          • Yellow: quasi-identifying or commercially sensitive.
          • Red: regulated data, PII, secrets, exploits, or legal exposure.
          These labels will not catch everything, so human review is still encouraged.
